# Open Source Cola (LabCoatz Edition)

**A scientifically reconstructed Coca-Cola clone based on chemical analysis.**

> **Origin:** This recipe was reverse-engineered by [LabCoatz](https://www.youtube.com/@LabCoatz) using mass spectrometry to identify the specific chemical flavor profile of modern Coca-Cola.
>
> 📺 **Watch the full explanation here:** [Perfectly Replicating Coca Cola (It Took Me A Year)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TDkH3EbWTYc)

## ⚠️ Safety Warning
**READ BEFORE PROCEEDING:**
1.  **Phosphoric Acid (85%):** This is corrosive. It can burn skin and damage eyes. **Wear gloves and eye protection.** Do not taste the undiluted acid.
2.  **Tea Tree Oil:** While used here in microscopic quantities for flavor, Tea Tree Oil can be toxic if ingested in large amounts. Ensure you are using **100% pure, high-quality oil** and adhere strictly to the dilution ratios. Do not consume undiluted essential oils.
3.  **Flammability:** High-proof food-grade alcohol is flammable. Keep away from open flames during mixing.

---

## The Science
This recipe differs from historical "Pemberton" recipes in three key ways to match **modern** Coca-Cola:
* **Wine Tannins:** Replicates the "dry" astringency of the coca leaf extract.
* **Tea Tree Oil:** Replaces Neroli to match the "green" terpene profile found in chemical analysis.
* **Acid Hydrolysis:** Boiling the sugar with phosphoric acid inverts the sugar (splitting sucrose into glucose/fructose), mimicking the taste of HFCS and "aging" the oils instantly.

## The Recipe

### Part 1: The "7X" Flavor Concentrate
*This creates the concentrated flavor oil. It produces enough oil for over 5,000 Liters of soda.*

**Ingredients:**
* **Lemon Oil:** 45.8 ml
* **Lime Oil:** 36.5 ml
* **Orange Oil:** 1.2 ml
* **Tea Tree Oil:** 8 ml *(The "Secret" Ingredient)*
* **Cassia Cinnamon Oil:** 4.5 ml
* **Nutmeg Oil:** 2.7 ml
* **Coriander Oil:** 0.7 ml
* **Fenchol:** 0.6 ml *(Note: Melts just above room temp; warm if needed)*
* **Food-grade Alcohol:** For dilution

**Instructions:**
1.  Combine all essential oils and Fenchol.
2.  (Optional but recommended) Let the mixture age for 24-48 hours.
3.  **To make the usable 7X Solution:** Take **20 ml** of this oil mixture and dilute it to **1 Liter** using food-grade alcohol.

---

### Part 2: The Water-Based Solution
*Adds color, mouthfeel, and the "bite."*

**Ingredients:**
* **Hot Water:** ~200 ml (for dissolving)
* **Vinegar (5%):** 10 ml
* **Caffeine:** 9.65 g
* **Glycerin:** 175 g *(Crucial for mouthfeel/thickness)*
* **Phosphoric Acid (85%):** 45 ml
* **Wine Tannins:** 8 g *(Dissolve thoroughly)*
* **Vanilla Extract:** 10 ml *(Or 1ml of a 9.5g/L Vanillin solution)*
* **Caramel Color (Shanks or similar):** 320 ml
* **Water:** To top off

**Instructions:**
1.  In ~200ml hot water, add ingredients one by one, dissolving fully between additions.
2.  Once dissolved, add water to reach a final volume of **1 Liter**.

---

### Part 3: Mixing The Syrup (The Final Step)
*Yields: 1 Liter of finished Cola*

**Ingredients:**
* **Sugar:** 104 g
* **Water:** Minimal amount (just enough to dissolve sugar)
* **Water-Based Solution (from Part 2):** 10 ml
* **7X Alcohol Solution (from Part 1):** 1 ml
* **Cold Carbonated Water:** To fill

**Instructions:**
1.  **The Acid Cook:** In a glass vessel (do not use metal), combine the Sugar, minimal Water, 10ml Water-Based Solution, and 1ml 7X Solution.
2.  **Heat:** Microwave or heat until nearly boiling.
    * *Why?* This accelerates the reaction between the phosphoric acid and sugar (inversion) and breaks down the citrus oils into new compounds.
3.  **Cool:** Cover immediately (to trap volatiles) and let cool completely.
4.  **Finish:** Pour the syrup into a bottle and top up with cold carbonated water.
5.  **Rest:** For best results, let the finished bottle sit in the fridge for 24 hours.
